# BRIEF: Kitty Chawla portfolio

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** Kitty supplied: graphic designer, brand strategist and writer; "dynamic yet playful with certain editorial touch for my writings"; build the page. Work was read from her public Drive folder (Design, Published Designs, Writings). No interview beyond that. Evidence vs assumption is marked below.

## The eight topics
1. **Vibe:** sticker-book, loud, warm, funny; then a hush into print. References (authored): a kirana-shop calendar, a hand-printed zine, a Sunday magazine supplement.
2. **Journey:** hello (name + her work as stickers) → what she does → brand worlds (HomeKrafted, Cent Spices, Indian Essence, public safety, corporate range) → how she thinks → writing → say hello.
3. **Energy:** loud hero, loud HomeKrafted, hot Cent Spices, calm Indian Essence, punchy public-safety pile, brisk corporate range, strategy as a breath, **hushed writing**, warm close.
4. **Feeling curve + peak:** see below.
5. **Something no site does:** the page *turns into a printed magazine*. A paper layer opens in a circle and the playful grotesque gives way to a reading serif.
6. **Aesthetic family:** playful maximalist for work, editorial for writing.
7. **One world or scenes:** distinct scenes (each brand gets its own colour and behaviour).
8. **Assets:** her real designs and phone screenshots of published posts (evidence), her poem (transcribed from Poetry.png), titles of her writings (from filenames).
