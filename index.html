Cent Spices

Turn every spice into a question people want answered. Four story formats, each built to be answered in one swipe.

  • What happens when…Cumin hits hot oil. Fennel after a meal. Mustard seeds crackling. The curiosity hook behind the live stories.
  • Myth vs FactTen of them. “Black pepper only adds heat.” Fact: it also helps nutrient absorption.
  • Did you know?Cloves are dried flower buds. Cinnamon is inner bark. Fenugreek turns mildly sweet when roasted.
  • Historically…Pepper was once called black gold. Wars were fought over cloves. Bay leaves crowned Greek champions.
  • Smaller seriesThis spice secret. Tiny but mighty. Kitchen science. Flavour or function? The 10-second magic.

Indian Essence

Wellness content that teaches first, sells second, and ends every post with a one-word DM prompt.

  • Hormonal Balance Through YogaA five-post carousel. One asana per slide: Baddha Konasana, Setu Bandhasana, Viparita Karani, Balasana, Supta Baddha Konasana.
  • DM “BALANCE”Every post closes with a keyword to message. A simple way to turn a reader into a conversation.
  • Did you know?Yoga comes from yuj, to unite. Surya Namaskar is a gratitude ritual, not just a warm-up.
  • Myth vs Fact“Yoga is only for flexible people.” Therapeutic yoga is for healing, not gymnastics.
  • Holi ideaA woman in yoga with chakra colours circling her, each colour's meaning in one line.

HomeKrafted

A homemade, youth-focused food brand for Tier-2 cities like Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula. I wrote the business model for three formats.

  • Food cartOne chef, one helper. Fastest breakeven, ideal for testing a market. Minimum monthly profit: ₹30,000 to ₹60,000.
  • KioskBest return in college and corporate hubs. Minimum monthly profit: ₹70,000 to ₹1.5 lakh.
  • CafeSit-down format for brand building, with higher ticket sizes. Minimum monthly profit: ₹1.2 to ₹2.5 lakh.
  • Conservative by designCosts, orders per day and average bill value all worked out with cautious assumptions.
